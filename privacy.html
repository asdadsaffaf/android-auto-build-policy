Android Auto Build

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

1. About this app

"Android Auto Build" is an internal tool operated by Funix Studio (“we”, “us”). It runs on our own build server and uploads the Android build files of our games (APK and AAB files) to a Google Drive folder that belongs to Funix Studio, so that our team and testers can download them. It is not offered to the public, and it is only used with Google accounts controlled by Funix Studio.

2. Google user data we access

When an authorized member of Funix Studio connects a Google account to "Android Auto Build", the app requests access to Google Drive. We use this access only to:

  • create folders for our builds inside the designated Google Drive folder;
  • upload the build files produced by our build server;
  • read the basic details (name, size, file ID) of the files the app has uploaded, to confirm the upload;
  • create a “view” link for an uploaded build file so our team can download it.

The app does not open, read, change or delete any other files in the Google Drive account, and it does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

3. Information we collect

"Android Auto Build" does not collect personal information. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. The files it handles are our own build files, not user content.

To stay connected to Google Drive, the app keeps the OAuth access token issued by Google. This token is stored only on our build server, in a configuration file that only the build account can read. It is never sent anywhere except to Google when the app calls the Google Drive API.

4. How we use and share information

  • Download links of uploaded builds are posted only to our internal team channels.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI or machine learning models.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties, except as required by law.

"Android Auto Build"’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • The OAuth token is kept on our build server until access is revoked or the app is removed.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the app can no longer access Google Drive and the stored token stops working.
  • Build files stay in our Google Drive until our team deletes them.

6. Security

All communication with Google uses HTTPS. The build server and its configuration files are only accessible to authorized members of Funix Studio.

7. Children

"Android Auto Build" is an internal business tool and is not directed to children.

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. The current version is always available at this address.
